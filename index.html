Mission

To help Somali residents build stable lives and succeed in society while keeping their cultural traditions.

Who am I that made this page?

Matt Schober, believe it or not, is a member of the Somali community! His cousin (those pesky cousins) joined the community. Matt has 35 blood-related cousins, a very large family, and feels that he relates to many Somalis in this way. Now, the Schober family is ingrained with Somali here in Minnesota! How incredible is that. He’s also visited with and dated those in the community. There are so many stories to tell.

Concerned About Waymo

We are still a long ways away from full self driving to travel here and there in Minnesota. And remember, we have SNOW! It’s a consideration, but I don’t see Waymo replacing drivers here anytime soon.

Somali’s and Technology

Many in our Somali community here in Minnesota are top users of technology, through mobile apps and driving technologies. Well, what comes next will be incredible, because by running an AI layer on top of the data, mobile apps and user interfaces will transition directly to what we want to see using SI (super-intelligence). Those apps we use will soon be changing in big ways!
